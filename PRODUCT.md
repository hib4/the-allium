# The Allium

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite; PixiJS WebGL rendering with a shared-choreography animated Canvas 2D fallback.

## Users

Deaf and hard-of-hearing concert audiences at Glitz Inclusive, Jakarta; visual operators; optional mobile companions.

## Product Purpose

Translate musical rhythm, pitch, intensity and expression into an identifiable shared visual score with captions.

## Operating Context

Panoramic 5:1 LED concert display; same-browser operator and display tabs; independent mobile visual-only demo. Performance must not require public internet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five routes, five instrument languages, scene focus modes, synthetic musical events, manual captions. No audio analysis, source separation, live transcription, venue relay or cross-device sync in v1.

## Brand Commitments

The Allium; warm, artistic, inclusive, editorial. Supplied navy, blue, teal, pink, violet and yellow palette. Living musical lines, not an equalizer or dashboard. Original logo unavailable: use temporary text identity and reserve logo placement.

## Evidence on Hand

User-provided brief and example copy; no original logo, event dates, artist lineup or testimonials supplied. Demo lyrics are sample content.

## Product Principles

Music determines motion. Instrument identity survives grayscale. Captions remain readable. Controls report truthful state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 principles; keyboard access, reduced motion, high contrast, no rapid full-screen flashes.
